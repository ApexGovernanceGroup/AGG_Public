File Naming Convention

Make the filename carry retrieval, authority, automation, and lifecycle state.

COMPO_DIV_FUNCTION_Title_DTG__Author_LC.ext

Token Purpose Example
COMPO Component, company, command, or major operating body. AGG
DIV Division, directorate, portfolio, service line, or echelon. OPS
FUNCTION Governed function, capability, or workstream. KM
Title Machine-safe title in PascalCase or hyphenated form. TaxonomyControlPlan
DTG UTC date-time group for sorting, provenance, and emerging-technology parsing. 20261007T1522Z
Author Author, steward, or approved owner code. BB
LC Lifecycle code in N.n format. 1.0

Example: AGG_OPS_KM_TaxonomyControlPlan_20261007T1522Z__BB_1.0.docx

Lifecycle Delineation

Every artifact needs a visible N.n lifecycle state.

  1. 0.1 | Intake

    A need, record, file, product, or evidence item enters the system.

    Control question: Who owns it and why does it exist?

  2. 0.2 | Classify

    Apply Level 1 and Level 2 taxonomy, owner, sensitivity, and retrieval metadata.

    Control question: Can a future user find it without knowing who created it?

  3. 0.3 | Draft

    Work is being built and should not be treated as approved guidance or authority.

    Control question: Is status obvious enough to prevent accidental operational use?

  4. 0.4 | Review

    The right owner, stakeholder, counsel, or authority reviews the content.

    Control question: What proves the right people reviewed the right version?

  5. 1.0 | Approve and Publish

    The approved version becomes active, discoverable, and usable.

    Control question: Where is the authoritative copy?

  6. 1.1 | Operate and Improve

    The artifact is used, measured, corrected, and improved through a review rhythm.

    Control question: What signal tells the owner to update it?

  7. 9.0 | Archive or Dispose

    The item is retained, archived, superseded, or disposed under the applicable rule.

    Control question: Which retention or continuity rule governs final handling?
